These are the colours that best match your design. Click Change to pick another yarn colour, or click the colour next to it to restore the matched colour.

    All yarns might not be needed for the best result. Test by removing one. The check is not 100 % accurate: sometimes more yarns give the most detailed result, so compare and restore if it looked better.

    To paint in missed stitches, pick a letter in the “Select to paint” section below (or press 1–9 or A–T, Esc to stop) and paint in the chart. Right-click (on touch: press and hold) restores the original stitch. Ctrl+Z undoes. ⇄ (S) flips stitches to the other yarn.
